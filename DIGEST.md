### Bybit sues North Korea and Lazarus Group under RICO, wins federal court freeze on stolen assets
*Global · 7 August 2026 · High impact*
> **So what:** This is a new private-sector recovery lever alongside sanctions and law-enforcement seizure: a victim exchange using civil RICO and a preliminary injunction to freeze traced assets held by intermediaries, without waiting on a government enforcement action. Banks assessing DPRK/Lazarus-linked wallet exposure should expect more of this pattern — private litigation reaching further downstream through laundering chains than OFAC designations alone — and should treat a named 'John Doe' defendant in this case as a live freeze order, not just a screening flag.
Source: [PR Newswire — Bybit Sues North Korea and Lazarus Group, Secures Preliminary Injunction Freezing Stolen Assets](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bybit-sues-north-korea-and-lazarus-group-secures-preliminary-injunction-freezing-stolen-assets-in-landmark-crypto-asset-recovery-effort-302846491.html)

### Putin signs Russia's first comprehensive crypto law, licensing exchanges and custodians under central-bank oversight
*Russia · 4 August 2026 · High impact*
> **So what:** This closes the 'pending signature' gap flagged in the prior entry: the licensing and custody framework that Sberbank, VTB, T-Bank and the Moscow Exchange have been building toward is now law, not a formality-in-waiting. From 1 September, correspondent, liquidity or CASP exposure touching any Russian-licensed exchange or custodian should be treated as direct Russia sanctions-nexus exposure, and screening logic keyed off 'pending legislation' caveats should be updated to reflect an enacted regime.
Source: [crypto.news — Putin signs law opening regulated crypto trading in Russia](https://crypto.news/putin-signs-law-opening-regulated-crypto-trading-in-russia/)

### OFAC formally designates Shelbit and Aban Tether under 'Economic Fury' — the Reuters-flagged Dubai exchange now sanctioned
*UAE / Iran · 7 August 2026 · High impact*
> **So what:** This closes the loop on the Shelbit case and sets a marker banks should watch: the interval from public reporting on an unlicensed, regulator-flagged intermediary to its formal OFAC designation can now be as short as seven days. Screening programmes that only re-run SDN lists on a periodic batch cadence, rather than triaging credible investigative reporting on flagged-but-undesignated counterparties in near real time, are structurally a week or more behind enforcement.
Source: [US Department of the Treasury — Treasury Sanctions Crypto Exchanges Funding Iran's IRGC and Enabling Illicit Finance](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0598)

### Trezor fulfilment-partner breach exposes shipping data of ~14,000 hardware-wallet customers
*Global · 13 August 2026 · Medium impact*
> **So what:** A shipping-address leak for hardware-wallet buyers is a targeting list for two distinct attack types a bank's fraud team should watch for: phishing impersonating Trezor, a bank or an exchange to harvest seed phrases, and physical 'wrench attack' targeting of known self-custody holders at a real home address. Customers who bought a hardware wallet in this window and then report unusual account-recovery or wire-transfer pressure warrant faster escalation, since the exposed data gives an attacker a verified name, address and device-ownership signal to build a convincing pretext around.
Source: [Trezor — Recent customer data exposed in shipping provider incident](https://trezor.io/blog/news/recent-customer-data-exposed-in-shipping-provider-incident)

### Seoul court sentences Delio CEO to 15 years for $49m crypto-lending fraud
*South Korea · 13 August 2026 · Medium impact*
> **So what:** A 15-year custodial sentence, even after part of the evidence was thrown out on procedural grounds, is a heavier outcome than most Western collapsed-lender cases (Celsius, Voyager) have produced, underlining Seoul's tougher post-Terra/FTX stance on crypto-lender misconduct. For a bank assessing counterparty risk on Korean crypto platforms, the case also confirms that filing false documents to obtain VASP registration is independently prosecutable in South Korea, separate from the underlying investor fraud.
Source: [The Block — South Korea sentences Delio CEO to 15 years in prison for $50 million crypto fraud: report](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-08-13-south-korea-sentences-delio-ceo-411688)

### CFTC and SEC file parallel civil charges over $397m Goliath Ventures crypto Ponzi scheme
*US · 11 August 2026 · Medium impact*
> **So what:** A three-agency stack, DOJ guilty plea first, CFTC and SEC civil actions following, on a single DeFi-liquidity-pool pitch shows regulators treating fabricated yield claims as straightforward fraud regardless of how the underlying token is eventually classified. For a bank's fraud and AML teams, a $397m scheme built on 1,600+ retail customers is a reminder that unverifiable 'liquidity pool' or 'DEX yield' language in a customer's stated investment purpose is a red flag worth screening for on its own, ahead of any CFTC/SEC classification outcome for the specific token.
Source: [CFTC — CFTC Charges Goliath Ventures Inc. and CEO with $400 Million Fraud Scheme](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9280-26)
